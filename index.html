About The College

Bhai Mati Dass College of Nursing & Ayurved stands as a beacon of healthcare education, committed to nurturing skilled, compassionate professionals in nursing, ayurved and allied healthcare. Rooted in the values of सेवा (service), स्वास्थ्य (health) and संस्कार (values), the college blends academic excellence with practical, hands-on training to prepare students for meaningful careers in India and abroad.

Punjab Nurses Registration Council Affiliated
Baba Farid University of Health Sciences Affiliated
Indian Nursing Council Recognized
Punjab State Faculty of Ayurveda & Unani System of Medicines Affiliated

Our Programmes

Choose Your Path

Every course is a doorway — to healing, to purpose, and to a career that truly matters.

Nursing

General Nursing & Midwifery (GNM)

A 3-year diploma course in nursing — especially beneficial for students looking to settle abroad after graduation.

Duration: 3 Years
Eligibility: 10+2, all streams · Age 17–35
Scholarships applicable · Contact for fees
Nursing

Auxiliary Nursing Midwifery (ANM)

A 2-year diploma programme focused on maternal health, child care and community health promotion at the grassroots level.

Duration: 2 Years
Eligibility: 10+2, all streams · Age 17–35
Scholarships applicable · Contact for fees
Advanced Nursing

Post Basic B.Sc. Nursing

A 2-year undergraduate bridge degree programme designed for GNM diploma holders to upgrade their professional qualifications.

Duration: 2 Years
Eligibility: 10+2 and GNM holder
Scholarships applicable · Contact for fees
Ayurveda

D.Pharma Ayurved

A 2-year diploma in Ayurvedic Pharmacy — bridging traditional Vedic healing sciences with pharmaceutical practice.

Duration: 2 Years
Eligibility: 10+2, all streams
Contact for fees
